Stereotype Judge Pipeline

How one sentence becomes a stereotype / anti-stereotype prediction today, and where accuracy stands. The current best (stacked ensemble, 0.813) ranks the stereotype sentence above its partner in 89% of pairs.

Every number is mean accuracy over the same 5 grouped folds (both halves of a pair always in the same fold).

Pipeline

src/merge.py 1 · Data: 1,518 sentences in 759 pairs CrowS-Pairs gender (262 pairs) + StereoSet inter (242) + intra (255). Label 1 = stereotype, 0 = anti-stereotype. 50/50 in every source.
src/split.py 2 · Split: 5 grouped folds Both sentences of a pair, and pairs sharing a sentence, stay in the same fold. Train on 4 folds, test on 1, rotate.
3 · Features, computed per sentenceconcatenated side by side
TF-IDF Words + word pairs Unigrams and bigrams, stop words kept (removing them cost 1.6 pts).
gender × word Word tagged with the sentence's gender she is nurturing → F_nurturing
embedding Snowflake-arctic-L 1024 numbers summarising meaning. Frozen; beat BERT, RoBERTa, mpnet.
masked LM roberta-large gender agreement [MASK] is nurturing → P(she) vs P(he), × actual gender
gender One-hot: F / M / both / none Detected from a word list.
LLM · Qwen 7B + 14B Gender-swap likelihood P(she is nurturing) vs P(he is nurturing)
LLM · Qwen 7B + 14B Zero-shot judge Yes-vs-no score for “Does this sentence express a gender stereotype?” Strongest single feature (7B: 0.69 pairwise).
experiments/topk.py 4 · Top-k selection + logistic regression (C = 1) Keeps the k TF-IDF features most linked to the label (chi²); k is chosen inside each training fold. Dense features are always kept. Trained on the 4 training folds only.
5 · Fine-tuned models, trained alongsiderunning on lisplab1 GPUs
done · 0.806 Qwen2.5-14B + LoRA (4-bit) Small adapter layers trained on our folds, up to 6 epochs. Strongest single model. 7B with 8 epochs and 3 seeds: 0.760.
stopped roberta-large, full fine-tune Failed to learn on fold 0 (0.507, validation at chance), a known instability on small data. Stopped.
experiments/ensemble.py 6 · Post-training: combine the models → 0.813 Stack the held-out P(stereotype) from logistic regression, random forest, 7B LoRA and 14B LoRA with a small logistic regression fitted on the training folds. Averaging LR + 14B alone, with nothing fitted, gives 0.810.
7 · Output: P(stereotype) per sentence → label 1 or 0 Scored against the held-out fold, then averaged over the 5 folds.

Accuracy so far

Bars start at 0.5 because a balanced binary task scores 0.5 by guessing. Fold-to-fold spread is about ±0.02, so differences under 2 points are not reliable. Fine-tuned BERT reached 0.619 at 7–8 epochs, but that epoch count was picked by looking at the test folds.

Why single sentences stay below 0.70

Pairs are near-identicalPartner sentences have cosine similarity 0.82–0.94 but opposite labels.
Labels are relative“Stereotype” means more stereotypical than the partner, which a single-sentence model never sees.
Knowledge is missingKnowing “nerd” is male-coded can't be learned from 1,200 sentences. Small masked LMs know it only weakly (0.57).
Noisy labelsSeveral mislabeled or non-gender pairs turned up in a random sample of 12.
